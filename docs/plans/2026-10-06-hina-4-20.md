# 4-20 · 山麓厄神之路

Six dry lanes on a wooded mountain terrace, unlocked after 4-19. The background rises into a forest hillside; moss, retaining stones and earth squares distinguish the board from a lawn. There is no pool, thick fog or visibility restriction.

## Encounter and source fidelity

Hina Kagiyama appears as both road and finale boss. The road uses a fresh weakened role, a brief cosmetic fade-and-return prelude with a visible spiral marker, then one mandatory spell: TH10 011/012 Bad Fortune on E/N, 013/014 Biorhythm of the Misfortune God on H/L. Original gameplay guides establish the road spell and preceding kedama encounter, but do not establish literal invisibility as a canon combat mechanic; the fade is this game's visual adaptation. It never hides live attacks or prevents targeting indefinitely.

The finale starts only after the road is cleared, with a new UID and full health. Three canon spells retain their preceding nonspells: Broken Amulet/Broken Charm (015–018), Misfortune's Wheel/Old Lady Ohgane's Fire (019–022), Pain Flow/Exiled Doll (023–026). E/N/H/L add 1/2/3/4 explicitly original cards, giving 4/5/6/7 finale stages. Existing Touhou declarations, damage floors and segment HUD remain authoritative. The road HUD explicitly labels a spell rather than a nonspell.

Hina collects misfortune; she is not herself unlucky. Her sprite stays upright while supplied spin/cast poses, width changes, orbiting ribbons, spiral seals and afterimages convey rotation. The supplied road BGM plays throughout the road encounter. Only the actual final boss switches to the supplied ending BGM.

## Original PvZ interactions

1. Delayed-action curse: a few planting cells show a 1.2-second warning, then temporarily slow native, fusion and support action clocks. No permanent changes or stacked penalties.
2. Misfortune dolls: at most three targetable dolls appear after warnings. Destroying one clears its row, releases a bounded local strike against ordinary enemies and returns 25 sun once. Expiry and owner/phase cleanup award nothing.
3. Unreliable magazine: marked cells weaken every third emitted ordinary projectile by 25%, deterministically and once per projectile. Ultimates remain counters. Clear marks with row plant food or an earned ultimate.
4. Final rotation combines these mechanisms while retaining their caps, warning windows and cleanup.

The enemy route focuses on kedama and star fairies, plus cone/bucket/brick/kabuto variants of both. Equipment stays independently destructible, fairies retain their warned star attack, and fused kedama split into two ordinary mini kedama once. Reinforcements continue during both boss roles; existing equipment-fusion zombies add variety. E/N/H use a dry-land conveyor, L uses manual selection and fusion.
