# 3-25 · 蓬莱人形 — design and implementation

User scope: TH08 Extra, two difficulties, half-beast Keine midboss and Mokou finale; supplied 24-pose sheets and two BGM files; midnight bamboo. No balloons, diggers or ranged ordinary enemies. Ground moon rabbits allowed.

Use existing EX / EX+ selection. Keep all 3 Keine and 11 Mokou stage spell cards in both modes, including the optional original-game Last Spell as an always-playable finale here. Preserve named pattern identity while adapting aimed shots to stationary plants and telegraphed grid hazards. EX+ adds distinct original moves in addition to stronger tuning. All boss sprites share the existing calibrated 180px body-height system.

Canonical sources: [spell list](https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Imperishable_Night/Spell_Cards/Extra), [Extra replay guide](https://extra.ohuda.com/th08.html), [pattern walkthrough](https://wikiwiki.jp/thk/%E6%B0%B8/EX).

- Keine: Old History's intersecting rice curtains; Ichijo bridge's opposing rotating red/blue streams; Next History's tighter curtains with aimed volleys.
- Mokou: Iwakasa rotating rice/redirecting knives; phoenix wing volleys and lingering feathers; temple reflected ofuda; Xu Fu's opposed talisman lanes; Honest Man's aimed shots and sweep with a visible safe side; Wu's drifting familiars and delayed bursts; phoenix-tail flame fans; Fujiyama warned eruption; Possessed by Phoenix timed multi-stage survival; Hourai Doll contracting red/blue orbits; Imperishable Shooting expansion/contraction/release waves.
- Original adaptations: warned left/right flame-ping-pong paddles with changing return lanes and finite rallies; ember plots extinguished by ice attacks; EX+ adds recorded-history plant silence for Keine, Mokou's warned rebirth circles and a double-ball rally. Mirror reed can return the ordinary boss bullets; ice, walls, healing and shoveling give counterplay to tile hazards.
- Ordinary enemy allowlist is enforced for authored road events, difficulty reinforcements, support spawns and boss summons. Midboss blocks finale until all mandatory cards finish. Pause, phase completion, owner death and retry clean up all hazards.
