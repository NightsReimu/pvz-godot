# 8-6 伊吹萃香终末酒宴

Goal: Add a complete four-difficulty TH7.5-inspired final stage using the supplied Suika sheet and final BGM, then publish v1.0.168.

Architecture: Add dedicated spell data, danmaku and boss runtime modules. Keep phase health gates, difficulty selection, conveyor/manual selection, collisions and music on the existing Touhou infrastructure. Wine slows the action delta passed to native plants and fusion components, while status expiry continues in real time. All fields, captured peas and miniature Suikas have a boss owner and clean up at phase end, retreat, death or restart.

Tech stack: Godot 4.6 / GDScript; Python, OpenCV and Pillow for lossless source sprite ownership; GitHub Actions for Windows/macOS/Web/Android releases.

## Source boundary

- Original developer: https://tasofro.net/touhou/
- Original TH7.5 spell list transcription: https://thwiki.cc/东方萃梦想/伊吹萃香
- Licensed character description: https://danmaku.jp/archive/character/c033/
- Character and original setting references: https://thwiki.cc/伊吹萃香

The six story cards are 符之一「投掷的天岩户」, 符之二「坤轴的大鬼」, 符之三「追傩返黑洞」, 鬼火「超高密度磷祸术」, 疎符「六里雾中」, and 「百万鬼夜行」. Their names and order remain identical across four difficulties; no later-game card is presented as a TH7.5 card. Density, mist, giant and miniature bodies, oni strength and the inexhaustible 伊吹瓢 inform the adaptation. Tower-defense targeting and original cards are explicitly adaptations, not claims about original mechanics.

## Encounter and counterplay

- Stage 8-6 follows 8-5. Five carved-stone lanes become a purple dusk banquet with lanterns and drifting mist. Ancient enemies form the road section; a weak Suika road preview retreats, then a fresh full-health finale starts the supplied music.
- Easy / Normal / Hard use a curated conveyor; Lunatic uses manual seed selection. Four tiers progressively increase density and speed, reduce warnings only down to readable minimums, and add original interludes.
- Canonical rocks: individual landing rings, descending stone silhouettes, delayed localized impact and chips.
- Giant: smoothly enlarged source sprite and telegraphed alternating stomps, not instant row deletion.
- Black hole: visibly gathers orbiting bullets, then releases rotating sectors.
- Dense fire: compressed flame beads split into visible sparks after a delay.
- Mist: translucent body and small tangible Suikas in marked lanes; all small bodies are targetable by plants and mowers.
- Million Oni finale: cumulative miniature parade and density/fire waves; the last declared card is always the canonical finale.
- Original 酿符「伊吹瓢的青石酒河」: one warned lane; the gourd grows and tips, amber wine advances right to left. Affected plants work at 55% speed for a bounded duration. Both layers and fused material actions are covered. 茉莉花茶 / 金铲铲牛奶 protect their surrounding 3×3 cells; activating a plant ultimate clears wine from its lane. Other lanes stay usable.
- Normal+ original 萃豆「弹仓酿成的鬼火」: a marked knot gathers at most eight ordinary forward pea shots in the right third. The knot's small, destructible body can be shot before a timed release. Lobbed shots, beams and ultimates are not captured.
- Hard+ original 宴阵「方圆三角的分酒席」: three chain weights mark separate cells; delayed circular, triangular and square patterns mix density effects with localized wine, leaving at least two lanes dry.
- Lunatic original 疎宴「一瓢分作百鬼席」: alternating mini-body lanes and staggered wine pours leave a readable dry lane. No off-screen or unannounced damaging hit.
