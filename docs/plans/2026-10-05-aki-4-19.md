# 4-19 · 红枫与丰穰

4-19 is a separate Touhou encounter after 4-18, rather than another fog backyard. Its six dry lanes occupy a red maple forest clearing. No water, mist, visibility restriction, or fourth-world weather is inherited.

## Encounter

- Shizuha Aki is the distinct, weakened road boss. Easy/Normal use a nonspell; Hard/Lunatic add TH10 001/002, Leaf Sign “Falling Leaves of Madness”.
- Minoriko Aki is the finale. TH10 003/004 versus 005/006 preserve the autumn-sky variants; 007/008 versus 009/010 preserve the harvester/grain-promise variants.
- The finale has 3/4/5/6 spell stages across Easy/Normal/Hard/Lunatic. Canon cards retain a short preceding nonspell. Each harder tier retains earlier original cards.
- Existing Touhou phase health bars, declarations, card gates, difficulty scaling and the v170 road/finale health balance apply. Ordinary land zombies continue reinforcing both encounters.
- Supplied road music starts with the level and remains through Shizuha. Only the actual Minoriko finale switches to the supplied ending music.

## Original board interactions

1. **秋收「红枫田垄的收获祭」** warns a few cells, briefly accelerates plant actions, and shows a final harvest warning before bounded local damage. Plant food clears the affected row.
2. **供奉「阳光贡仓的秋日祭」** creates targetable baskets after warnings. They provide limited healing to ordinary nearby zombies. Destroying one returns 25 sun once; expiry, card change and owner death award none.
3. **枫阵「交错六畦的红叶毯」** places brief leaf mats that slow actions on selected cells. Native, fusion and support plants use the same modifier. Row plant food clears them.
4. **丰穰「六畦同庆的秋日宴」** alternates these mechanisms, preserving hazard and basket limits.

Warnings remain visible on narrow six-row mobile boards. Ambient leaves are sparse, translucent and cosmetic; attack leaves have distinct outlines. Mechanisms respect time stop and clear on phase/owner/level changes.

## Art and level choices

The supplied 6×4 sprite sheets are already transparent. Import retains original RGBA pixels and each pose, calibrating a common foot anchor. The red-maple background is generated with the built-in imagegen tool; dry ochre planting squares and falling leaves are drawn separately, so the board remains legible.

Dry-land seed deliveries include repeaters, threepeaters, snow peas, melons, kernels, torchwood, nuts, pumpkins, healing gourds, umbrellas, starfruit, cactus, cherry bombs, jalapeños and blover. Reinforcements use ordinary walkers, cones, buckets, newspapers, screen doors, football zombies and occasional balloons. No aquatic or fog enemies are introduced. Easy/Normal/Hard retain the conveyor; Lunatic follows existing manual selection and fusion conventions.
