# 4-21 · 玄武之泽的河童

Six dry lanes on a rain-soaked basalt terrace, unlocked after 4-20. Genbu (玄武) puns on basalt: hexagonal column cliffs frame the board, the main waterfall pours behind the boss lane, and a kappa water wheel turns on the left bank. Rain is animated (thin streaks and puddle rings); there is no pool, thick fog or visibility restriction.

## Encounter and source fidelity

TH10 stage 3 (玄武の沢) has Nitori Kawashiro as both mid-boss and boss. The mid-boss appears in optical camouflage and fights from it; the original guide describes the card as enemy-dependent fixed shots. This stage adapts that literally for the road: a camouflaged nonspell (「光学迷彩下的偷袭」, about six seconds of refracted water shots) followed by the declared card 光学「光学迷彩」 (027/028, E/N) or 光学「水迷彩」 (029/030, H/L). The road keeps the weakened 9% health role; the declared card must finish even under burst damage.

While camouflaged (road, and finale cards 迷彩突击队/工房总动员) she is drawn as a faint bent-light silhouette with water rings and takes 70% damage, unless a plantern or reveal-trait fusion stands in her current lane. She is never untargetable.

The finale starts after the road retreats, with a fresh UID and full health. Each canonical group keeps its real difficulty split (numbers per TH10 spell practice):

Board adaptations: floods enter from the top and bottom board edges and bend toward the house; 漂溺 surfaces glimmering bubbles that hold, then drift; 河口浪潮/山洪暴发 are tidal walls across all lanes with one open lane; 幻想大瀑布 pours columns down the board while a jet answers from the right; 妖怪黄瓜 fires cucumber bullets that re-aim at the nearest plant; 延展手臂 telegraphs arm beams into the busiest lanes; 回转顶板 spins two counter-rotating plate rings with a lane beam. Each canonical card keeps its preceding nonspell 「河童的水枪连射」. E/N/H/L add 1/2/3/4 original cards, and the shared finale module adds 水车「留出一格的六轮河童水车」 and 雨垄「瀑布倒灌的雨后河道」, giving 6/7/8/9 finale phases.

## Original PvZ interactions

1. 迷彩「光学迷彩突击队」: 2–3 fused enemies arrive in optical camouflage. Plants do not acquire them as lane targets until revealed by a plantern/reveal fusion in the lane or radius, a hit (2 s), or walking into the near half where rain smears the film. Shots already in flight still collide.
2. 胡瓜「玄武之泽的黄瓜诱饵」: warned cells grow up to four targetable cucumbers (kappa bait, 260 + 30 × rank health). They draw their lane's fire; breaking one returns 15 sun once. A zombie that reaches it first eats it, healing 20% and taking 20% less damage for 6 s. Expiry and owner/phase cleanup pay nothing.
3. 水压「河童重工高压水炮」: 1.4 s row warning, then a jet sweeps right-to-left: light damage (30 + 8 × rank before difficulty scaling; shooters have 120 health) and a 6 s soak that slows native, fusion and support action clocks to 70%. A soaked torchwood steams and cannot ignite peas until it dries. An umbrella leaf shelters its 3×3; row plant food or an earned ultimate clears soak and pending jets.
4. 河童「玄武工房总动员」 (final): camouflage squad, cucumbers and cannon together, still within their caps.

Enemy route: kedama and star fairies from TH10 stage 3, plus rain and engineering zombies. Most scripted arrivals and finale reinforcements are equipment fusions: cone/bucket/brick/kabuto kedama and fairies, cone/bucket umbrella, brick ladder, cone jack-in-the-box, kabuto ninja, bucket newspaper, cone digger, bucket pogo. Brick resists magnets, so the belt carries both magnet-shroom and raw damage. Plant pool: repeater/threepeater/snow/split peas, kernel and melon pults, wall-nut/tall-nut/pumpkin, torchwood (countered by the cannon), plantern (camouflage), umbrella leaf (cannon), healing gourd, magnet-shroom, starfruit, spikeweed (diggers), cherry bomb, jalapeño and jasmine tea. E/N/H use the conveyor (2.8–4.0 s), L uses manual selection with a sun producer.

## Balance record

Nitori's base health is 22,000 (Hina 24,000): camouflage, decoys and soak already add effective health. Full encounters use 1.25× density (Wind God finales 1.6×) because tidal walls and floods are not density-scaled. Final native zero-preplant routes (`nitori_balance_test.gd`, seed 906, campaign fusion enemies, real belt/paid planting and earned ultimates) all win with no mower used:

| Difficulty | Total | Finale | Phases | Lost plants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 667.9 s | 300.9 s | 6 | 0 |
| Normal | 856.7 s | 511.0 s | 7 | 4 |
| Hard | 900.7 s | 501.3 s | 8 | 14 |
| Lunatic | 1015.7 s | 633.0 s | 9 | 89 |

4-20 at v1.0.176 takes 544–852 s, so stage 3 is deliberately longer. A timeline probe (Easy) shows the extra pre-finale time comes from the camouflage road (49 s vs 27 s) and slower clearing of fusion-armored waves (119 s vs 73 s after the road), not from stalls. The boss-only prepared fixture (`--prepared`) is informational: it skips the stage build-up and Hina's own Hard fixture also loses at v1.0.176.
