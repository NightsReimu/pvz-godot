## Design

1. Waterfall road lasting about 300 authored seconds (plus a weak Momiji encounter near 40% progress); many pre/post-midboss waves. E/N/H conveyor 2.8–4.0s, L selected cards. Six water rows with sleep-free water shooters and lily-pad access; provide initial lily pads so the slow belt remains playable. River/aerial fused enemies during road, mountain fused armor waves during Aya. Enemies keep spawning during Boss, bounded to existing 42 live/queued support budget.
2. Momiji: weak phase health capped relative to Aya, two nonspell patterns, one patrol spell (plus an extra guard spell at H/L). Readable shield-facing defense, marked sword sweep, bounded guard protection; do not turn her into a full HP finale or an invisible Hina replacement.
3. On real Aya arrival: one atomic water→land terrain/grid change, short visually eased waterfall→mountainside transition, preserve UID/HP/charge/fusion and all crops above supports. Per the later user request, remove every lily pad from both board layers on Aya arrival, including later placements; retry restores exactly twelve initial pads. Aquatic plants already placed are acclimated for this encounter; subsequent legal land placement follows actual dry terrain. Existing swimmers survive and continue, water-only future card entries become compatible alternatives. Boss ending BGM starts only then, road track through the long road/Momiji.
4. Aya: rapid visible lane dashes through all six rows and planting columns1–8, continuing during every card and recovery window, with finite afterimages; rendering, collision target and danmaku origin share the current body position. A short approach telegraph precedes wind/sword/contact effects. Wind leaf veiling remains a visible leaf silhouette, revealed by plantern. Canonical branching crossed paths, spiraling leaves, accelerating wind, moving blockade walls and timed rapid-flight survival.
