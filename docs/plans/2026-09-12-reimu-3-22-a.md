# Reimu 3-22-a and Touhou balance implementation plan

**Goal:** Add the requested single Reimu encounter, all TH08 stage 4A difficulty variants, restrained night bamboo scenery, spell tiles, and gentler Touhou damage/density.

**Architecture:** Reuse level selection, phase gates, sprite caches, BGM, and the shared swept-segment danmaku runtime. Keep Reimu's canonical card table and patterns separate from original tower-defense tile effects. A dedicated runtime owns spell tiles, boss presentation, background lighting and cleanup.

**Tech stack:** Godot 4.6 / GDScript; existing asset pipeline; native screenshots and deterministic headless battle tests.

## Design decisions

- Level ID `3-22-a`, unlocked after `3-21`, six grass lanes, one Reimu spawn, no `mid_boss_kind`. Use supplied stage/boss MP3s.
- Preserve all stage 4A spells by folding Reimu's two mid-encounter spells into her single final encounter. Easy has five canonical cards, Normal/Hard/Lunatic six, with Normal+ Last Spell; no unrelated Last Word.
- IDs: Easy 055/059/063/067/071; Normal 056/060/064/068/072/075; Hard 057/061/065/069/073/076; Lunatic 058/062/066/070/074/077.
- Distinct straight/barrier streams, expanding multicolour dream orbs, enclosing ofuda arrays, converging/returning ofuda, nested danmaku barriers and Blink survival finale. Geometry/collision must agree. Pattern timing/counts are tower-defense adaptations, not a claim of frame-exact reproduction.
- Alternative of omitting the two midboss cards loses requested content; spawning a separate encounter violates the requested flow. Merge them into the finale.
- Original tile effects: red seal temporarily suspends plants (also blocks active skills), blue purification slows/damages enemies, gold barrier reduces plant damage. Warnings precede activation, limited simultaneous tiles, no permanent lockdown; stage change/death/retry clears owner effects.
- Preserve supplied sprite identity and 24-frame order. Imagegen background extraction attempt failed (opaque red backdrop); do not ship it. User subsequently provided the final RGBA sheet and requested using it; exact 256px cell extraction preserves all pixels and alpha.
- Lower all Touhou plant-damage multipliers by about 20%, reduce global density and speed pressure, lengthen wave cadence. Preserve family silhouettes under crowded rendering. Add measured collision benchmark and correctness coverage.
- Existing top difficulties continue to require seed selection, cost, cooldown, replay persistence and separate clear records.
- Night bamboo starts dim; boss arrival smoothly raises localized light. Keep board and UI legible at desktop and mobile sizes.
